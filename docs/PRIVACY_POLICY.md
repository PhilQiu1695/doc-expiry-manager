# Privacy policy — Doc Expiry

Last updated: May 9, 2026  

This policy describes how the **Doc Expiry** mobile app (“the app”) handles information. It is meant for App Store / Play listings and support pages. Host this file at a public URL (GitHub Pages, your domain, etc.) and link it in store consoles.

## Summary

- The app is built for **tracking document expiry dates on your device**.
- **Your documents and notes are stored locally** on the phone using on-device storage (AsyncStorage). They are **not sent to our servers** — we do not operate a backend that receives your document list in normal use.
- **Expiry reminders** are **scheduled locally** with the operating system’s notification APIs. Reminder text is composed on your device from the names and dates you entered.
- **Language and appearance preferences** are stored on the device.

## Data we do not collect (in normal app use)

We do **not** intentionally collect, sell, or rent your personal document contents through this app for analytics or advertising. There is no account login in the default setup described here.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the OS may show reminders around **30 days** and **7 days** before an expiry date you set. You can withdraw notification permission in system settings at any time; scheduling accurate reminders may depend on OS permission and battery optimizations.

## Third parties

The app uses standard platform components (e.g. Apple / Google notification and storage APIs). Those providers’ privacy terms apply to how the OS handles data. Refer to **Apple** and **Google** privacy policies for device-level behavior.

## Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the age required in your region). Do not enter personal data you are not allowed to store.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The “Last updated” date will change when it does.
